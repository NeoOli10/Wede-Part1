Our Story

Founded in 2022 by Katli Oliphant, a passionate sweet enthusiast, Katli’s Candy Shop began as a home-based venture selling handmade sour sweets and liquorice sweet sticks at local markets. With unique flavors and colorful presentation, the shop quickly gained popularity and opened its first brick-and-mortar store in the heart of Johannesburg in 2023. Today, we serve candy lovers across South Africa both in-store and online — bringing joy, quality, and creativity to every order.
